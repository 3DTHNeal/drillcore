# Northbridge Robotics: a year of slower sales

Everything here is fictional. It's a draft written from the example notes, in its own words, so the overlap check has
something to read.

Northbridge Robotics sold less in 2025 than the year before. Revenue slipped from $572.5M to $551.1M, a drop of a
little under four percent, and the gross margin narrowed by more than three points to 41.2%. The company ended the
year with 1,757 people on staff, some sixty fewer than a year earlier.

Spending on research came down too, to $77.3M. Against that, the order book offers some comfort: $95.4M of work is
waiting, and about $67.5M of it should turn into sales within the coming year.

The annual report frames the year as one of "discipline before growth", a phrase a reader may weigh against the
shrinking research budget.
