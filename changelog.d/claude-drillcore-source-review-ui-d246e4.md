### Added

- `drillcore review` opens a page in the browser, served on this computer only, that steps through every claim `check` judges, with the note on one side and the stored source it cites on the other. Each figure, quotation and worked-out figure is coloured by its verdict. The source opens at the passage that holds the claim, with the figure marked at whatever scale it was found, the row's words underlined near it, and the page or section a pinpoint cites shaded. A correction the source or the working itself gives is offered, to apply with one key and undo: the source's words for a quotation a word off, what a working gives, or the page a figure is really on. A claim can be marked as matching its source or as disagreeing with it, saying how, in the new `checks/review.csv`, and a mark asks to be looked at again once its line or source changes. A failure or a warning can be accepted with a reason, written to `checks/waivers.csv`, and a line can be edited in place, after which the checks run again. The example workspace is shown and never changed.
