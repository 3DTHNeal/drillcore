### Added

- `proof` has a `workspace figures` check. In the sections `[proof] workspace_sections` names, in a draft or a companion file (an evidence appendix, say), each figure about the workspace's own record must be its value: source entries and distinct addresses, fetches, documents with stored text, companies, tagged facts, the filings those facts came from and the filing-list entries, as the database gives them now; figures compared, matching, mismatched and with no fact, from the last `claims` run; judged, failed, waived, warnings, not judged, inputs to derived figures and lines citing no source, from the last `check` run. A figure that differs fails and says the value, naming the measure it is the value of if it is one ("700 filings" for the filings the facts came from, when 700 is the filing-list entries); an earlier `check` run's value is a warning naming that run; "sources" given as the count of entries rather than distinct addresses is a warning. A line labelled "Not judged:" or "Warnings:" must add up to the run's total, and one that leaves some out is a warning saying how many. Words that could mean anything ("filings", "sources", "companies") are judged only beside another measure, and a day of a date or a year is no count.
- `check` keeps its counts in the database (the `run_counts` table), with a digest of the notes it read, for the run `history` keeps. `proof` says when the notes have changed since, since a run's figures describe the notes as they were.
